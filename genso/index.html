What's this?

You may remember learning about the periodic table of elements in school science classes. The table organizes chemical elements according to their properties. Each element has its own distinctive characteristics. The GENSOGAKUSHO (© Agedoridori) project personifies these elements, creating character illustrations and imagining the world they inhabit.

GENSOGAKUSHO × GENSOGOSEI (nucleosynthesis) is a collaboration between the GENSOGAKUSHO artist and Nobuya Nishimura, a researcher in nuclear astrophysics. The illustrations depict these element characters taking part in astrophysical nucleosynthesis—how atomic nuclei are formed and transformed in the Universe. Drawing on research into astronomical phenomena and nuclear reactions, the characters bring nucleosynthesis to life with charm and humor.

The GENSOGAKUSHO world also incorporates stories about the discovery of elements and their chemical properties. The illustrations can therefore be enjoyed as part of a fictional world while also serving as useful material for scientific explanations.

For more information, visit the GENSOGAKUSHO website or see the presentation slides from the UKAKUREN workshop (July 2022).
